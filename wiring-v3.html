Why it works

“Every man can, if he so desires, become the sculptor of his own brain.”Santiago Ramón y Cajal, 1897

Pulling a memory out strengthens it more than rereading.Roediger & Karpicke, 2006

Spotting the word turned tiles into one chunk. A runner used that trick to go from 7 digits to 79.Ericsson, Chase & Faloon, 1980

Weeks of memory training change brain activity.Olesen, Westerberg & Klingberg, 2004

After sleep, people tapped a practised sequence about 20% faster, with no extra practice.Walker et al., 2002

Taxi drivers, pianists and jugglers all showed brain changes from practice.Maguire et al., 2000 · Bengtsson et al., 2005 · Draganski et al., 2004

You get better at what you practise. Wider benefits are still being studied.Melby-Lervåg & Hulme, 2013
