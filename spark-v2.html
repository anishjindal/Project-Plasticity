Why it works

Your brain just learned something new.

Making music ties hearing to movement. A rhythm you’ve never played makes that link do new work.Zatorre, Chen & Penhune, 2007 · Nature Reviews Neuroscience

Non-musicians learned a piano piece by ear in five days. After that, just hearing it lit up the movement areas of their brains.Lahav, Saltzman & Schlaug, 2007 · Journal of Neuroscience

Timing is trained, not born. Musicians tap with far less error than everyone else.Repp, 2005 · Psychonomic Bulletin & Review

New skills change the brain. Adults who learned to juggle grew grey matter in three months.Draganski et al., 2004 · Nature

One session won’t rewire you. Novelty is the point, so tomorrow the rhythm changes.Simons et al., 2016 · Psychological Science in the Public Interest
