# 100 ideas for Project Plasticity

Written 2026-11-06. Grouped by theme, ten per theme. The five starred (★) ones are the most different from each other and have interactive mockups in `ideas-mockups.html`.

## A. Your world becomes the gym (personalization)
1. ★ **Field feed.** Paste a paragraph from your own work (a pitch, a case, a score) and it becomes today's recall, attention or reasoning drill.
2. Calendar-aware sessions: a 3-minute speed drill before a meeting block, a longer reasoning set on a quiet afternoon.
3. "Standup recall": remember who said what from a mock standup in your own team's vocabulary.
4. Interest-specific vocabulary drills (biotech, jazz, law) for memory span.
5. A founder mode where exercises use real-looking metrics dashboards.
6. Medical mode: differential-diagnosis reasoning puzzles.
7. Athlete mode: reaction and visual-tracking drills with sport-shaped stimuli.
8. Artist mode: visual-processing drills built from colour, proportion and negative space.
9. Language-learner mode: listening span in the language you're learning.
10. "Bring your own material": upload lecture slides; the app builds spaced recall from them.

## B. Off-screen reps (physical world)
11. ★ **Daily dare.** One small real-world novelty a day (other hand, new route, eyes-closed shower) with a one-tap "Did it".
12. Walk-and-think: a 10-minute audio prompt sequence for a walk.
13. Juggling / balance progressions tracked by self-report.
14. Handwriting with the non-dominant hand, photographed weekly.
15. "Talk to a stranger" social-novelty prompts.
16. Cook a recipe without reading it twice.
17. Navigate home without maps once a week.
18. Learn a 30-second piano or drum pattern.
19. Reading-aloud reps for articulation and attention.
20. A "no-autopilot" morning: three routines done differently.

## C. See your brain change (progress)
21. ★ **Living brain record.** The onboarding brain becomes your record: each pathway thickens with practice; scrub back through weeks.
22. A weekly "what wired" letter, written plainly.
23. Switch-cost, span and reaction-time trend lines with honest noise bands.
24. Before/after replay: watch your own first round next to today's.
25. Personal bests that decay if unused, like real skill.
26. A "consolidation meter" that fills with sleep and spacing, not just reps.
27. Plateaus explained, not hidden: "this is normal in week 3".
28. Export a one-page progress PDF for a coach or therapist.
29. Milestones tied to research-sized effects, not arbitrary badges.
30. A calm year view: 365 small dots, no streak shaming.

## D. Train together (social)
31. ★ **Duet.** Two brains side by side; you and a friend take asynchronous turns on the same round.
32. Team gym for startups: a shared weekly challenge, no leaderboard by default.
33. Teach-back: explain today's concept to a partner in 30 seconds, recorded.
34. Accountability pairs matched by goal and time zone.
35. Family mode with age-appropriate variants of the same exercise.
36. Co-op puzzles that need two people's partial information.
37. A "spotter" who can see your plan and nudge once a week.
38. Classroom mode for a professor to assign reasoning sets.
39. Anonymous percentile only, opt-in.
40. Share a blink card that shows a pathway, never a score.

## E. The day's rhythm (timing)
41. ★ **Night replay.** A 60-second evening wind-down that replays today's reps slowly, because sleep is when consolidation happens.
42. Morning 90-second primer before the first deep-work block.
43. Spaced-repetition scheduler across exercises, not just flashcards.
44. Micro-sessions that fit a kettle boil or an elevator ride.
45. A "rest day" the app proposes when performance dips.
46. Weekend longer session with a new exercise type.
47. Jet-lag mode that shifts the schedule gently.
48. Pre-exam taper: lighter, confidence-building sets.
49. Commute mode, audio only.
50. Pause-and-resume that remembers exactly where you were.

## F. The coach (AI)
51. A coach that re-asks the commitment question at week one and two.
52. "Why this exercise today" in one sentence, always visible.
53. Difficulty explained: "we're holding you near 80% right because…"
54. Weekly review conversation, five questions, plan retuned.
55. A coach voice you can pick: terse, warm, scientific.
56. Reflection prompt after each session: "what felt hard?"
57. Confusion detector: if you miss the instructions, it rewrites them.
58. Goal drift check: your actions vs your stated goals.
59. Plain-language research digest matched to what you practised.
60. "Ask the coach" with every answer cited or marked as opinion.

## G. New exercises (games)
61. Triage (built): task switching with messages.
62. Glimpse (built): speed of processing.
63. Wired (built): daily word connections.
64. Spot the diff on dashboards.
65. Dual n-back with your own field's icons.
66. Roadmap logic: ordering dependencies under time pressure.
67. Voice-note recall: listen once, answer three questions.
68. Peripheral vision sweep with moving targets.
69. Inhibition: a go/no-go with tempting-but-wrong answers.
70. Mental rotation using objects from your field.

## H. Make it honest (science and trust)
71. A "what this can and can't do" page in plain English, with citations.
72. Every claim links to a verified paper; every paper paired with a hedged claim.
73. Transfer-effect honesty: say when a skill is unlikely to generalise.
74. A "replication status" tag on each study cited.
75. A critics' corner: Simons et al. 2016 summarised fairly.
76. Your own n-of-1 experiment: A/B two schedules on yourself.
77. Effect sizes shown as "small / medium / large" with a sentence.
78. No medical claims; a clear line about when to see a clinician.
79. Open data export of everything you've done.
80. A changelog of what the adaptive algorithm changed for you and why.

## I. Make it felt (sensory and craft)
81. Haptic tick on each correct answer, with the same on/off toggle as sound.
82. A single hand-drawn motif unlocked per week, inside the brain.
83. Ambient sound beds per exercise type, very quiet.
84. Dark-room mode for night replay only.
85. Large-type and high-contrast variants of every exercise.
86. One-handed layouts on phone.
87. A printable paper edition of the week's exercises.
88. Screen-reader-first versions of the listening tasks.
89. A reduced-motion story that is as good as the animated one.
90. Widgets: today's rep on the lock screen.

## J. Growing the thing (product and business)
91. A free daily rep forever; a paid personalised plan.
92. Company plans with a privacy promise: the employer never sees scores.
93. University pilots with a professor dashboard.
94. A public "exercise lab" where new games are tested on volunteers.
95. Creator mode: anyone can author an exercise in their field.
96. Integrations that stay opt-in: calendar, sleep, Obsidian notes.
97. A research partnership so the data can answer real questions.
98. A newsletter of one verified finding a week.
99. A referral that gives a friend a Duet, not a discount.
100. A kill-switch: the app tells you when it's no longer helping you.
